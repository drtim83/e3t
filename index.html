E3T — Business Logic & Formulas Reference

Overview

E3T (Engagement Estimation & Evaluation Tool) is a resource rates and effort planning workbook. It calculates project cost, revenue, and margin based on labor rates, effort allocation, and commercial assumptions.

Tabs & Purpose

TabPurpose
ProjectProject metadata, schedule assumptions, and commercial terms (discount, allowance, risk reserve).
RatesLabor rate card — list price and standard cost per role (MYR/hour).
Effort60-month resource allocation grid. Enter person-months (PM) per resource per calendar month.
P&LProfit & Loss summary with resource-level, category-level, and key metrics.

Formulas

1. Sell Price

Sell Price = List Price × (1 − Global Discount %) × (1 + Global Allowance %)

The sell price is the effective billing rate after applying the negotiated discount and any contractual allowance uplift.

2. Total Person-Months (PM)

Total PM = Σ(monthly PM allocations for all 60 months)

Sum of effort values across all visible months for a given resource row.

3. Total Hours

Total Hours = Total PM × Working Hours per Month

Working Hours per Month is configured in the Project tab (default: 146 hrs/month).

4. Revenue

Revenue = Total Hours × Sell Price

5. Cost

Cost = Total Hours × Standard Cost Rate

Standard Cost Rate is sourced from the Rate Card tab per job code.

6. Gross Margin

Gross Margin (MYR) = Revenue − Cost
Gross Margin % = (Revenue − Cost) / Revenue

Delivery Margin % on the Effort tab is calculated at the individual resource level. Project-level margin aggregates all resources.

7. Risk Reserve

Risk Reserve (MYR) = Total Revenue × Risk Reserve %

Applied as a percentage of total revenue to account for project delivery risk.

8. Blended Rates

Blended Sell Rate = Total Revenue / Total Hours (across all resources)
Blended Cost Rate = Total Cost / Total Hours (across all resources)

Rate Card Logic

Each role in the Rate Card has:

  • List Price — published standard hourly rate (MYR/hr)
  • Std Cost Rate — internal cost to deliver (MYR/hr)
  • Delivery Margin % — (List − Cost) / List
  • Blended Average — arithmetic mean of all rates in the card

Effort Grid

Effort is entered in Person-Months (PM) per calendar month. A value of 1.0 represents one full resource for that month. Values may be fractional (e.g., 0.5 = half-time).

Each resource row pulls List Price and Cost Rate from the Rate Card based on the selected Job Code.
